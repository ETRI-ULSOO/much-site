## Inquiries

We welcome inquiries about using MUCH's research results, institutional cooperation, and media coverage. Please use the categories below, and the appropriate contact person will respond.

### Research and Technical Cooperation

Inquiries about the platform's features, data structure, or standards application.

### Data and Content Use

Inquiries from organizations that would like to use the datasets or the pilot content.

### Media and Press Materials

Interview requests and press-material requests from news organizations.
