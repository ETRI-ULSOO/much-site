## One-Paragraph Summary

CHIC was a project that researched technology for curating cultural heritage materials so that people could find them more easily. It ran from July 2020 to December 2022 as a designated culture-technology R&D project of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism. The idea of recording the relationships between artifacts as data, and letting users browse by following those relationships, originated in this project, and MUCH expanded that idea into standards and a platform.

## What Carried Over Into MUCH

### A Relationship-Centered Data Structure

CHIC set the direction of recording the relationships between artifacts, rather than recording each artifact on its own. MUCH's knowledge graph is the result of rebuilding that direction to align with CIDOC CRM, the international standard.

### The Need for Data-Creation Standards

Carrying out CHIC made it clear that integration was difficult because data formats differed from institution to institution. This is the background for why MUCH included establishing standards among its goals.
