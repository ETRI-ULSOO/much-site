## Coverage Overview

Evening news programs on national broadcasters, regional broadcasters, daily newspapers, and wire services covered MUCH's research results. By the count in the presentation materials, this happened more than twenty times.

The coverage falls into two periods. In January 2024, the digitally recreated Gwanggaeto Stele was reported. In late May 2024, following the demonstration at the National Museum of Korea, KBS, SBS, Daejeon MBC, TJB, TV Chosun, and Yonhap News TV each introduced the intelligent cultural-heritage platform. In July 2025, the release of the Cultural Heritage Digital Asset Standard Guidelines and the push for a TTA standard appeared in ZDNet Korea and The Herald Business, and ZDNet's "Digital K-Heritage" series also covered the guidelines in its National Museum of Korea installment. The ETRI webzine introduced the research as well.
