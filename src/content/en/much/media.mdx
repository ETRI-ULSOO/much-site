## Coverage Overview

Evening news programs on national broadcasters, regional broadcasters, daily newspapers, and wire services covered MUCH's research results. By the count in the presentation materials, this happened more than twenty times.

Page 70 of the final report separately lists four press items from the project's third stage: a July 2025 report related to the National Museum of Korea, a report on the release of the standard guidelines, an ETRI webzine article from August 2025, and a cultural-heritage feature article in ZDNet from the same month. However, the report does not give the article titles or web addresses either, so, as with the list below, these will be added once the addresses are confirmed.

## List of Press Coverage

So far, nine reports from eight outlets have been confirmed, and their web addresses and publication dates are being compiled. The list will be posted here once that work is complete.

The confirmed outlets are KBS, SBS, Daejeon MBC, TJB, TV Chosun, Yonhap News, Chungcheong Today, and Digital Times.
