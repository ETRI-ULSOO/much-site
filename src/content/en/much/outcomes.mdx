## Composable Datasets

The project produced 12,131 items, far exceeding the contractual target of 4,500. The data is organized into four categories.

### 3D Shape Data

Data capturing the three-dimensional shape of an artifact. It was created in glTF format and then recompressed into Nexus format so it can be opened directly on the web.

### RTI Data

A method that photographs a subject under light from many changing directions to capture even fine surface unevenness. It is used for subjects where texture matters more than shape, such as the inscriptions on a stele or traces on a metal surface.

### Gigapixel Images

An image containing hundreds of millions of pixels in a single photograph, so that fine detail holds up no matter how far it is zoomed in on screen.

### Metadata

Material that organizes an artifact's name, period, material, excavation site, and collection information into standard fields.

In Year 1, asset optimization was carried out for 56 artifacts and 66 components, divided into 60 items of 3D data, 15 of RTI, and 5 of gigapixel images.

### Expanded Training and Validation Dataset: 77,976 Items

Separately from the composable dataset, 77,976 items of data were collected to train and validate AI models (final report, p. 64, Phase 3 results). Its composition is as follows.

| Category | Count |
| --- | --- |
| OCR of exhibited-artifact information cards | 1,272 |
| Open API from the Culture Public Data Plaza | 2,065 |
| Artifacts from the National Museum of Korea website | 4,173 |
| Exhibition information | 626 |
| Test and validation | 69,840 |

With this data applied, the accuracy of semantic search rose 6.25 percentage points, from 62.58% to 68.83%. The Cultural Heritage Classification, Tagging, and Relationship Definition document was expanded to version 3.0, covering 1,714 instances and 5,926 items.

## Performance Metrics

The performance of the AI technology built into the platform was compared against the benchmark levels set in the contract. All eight items exceeded the benchmark (final report, p. 69).

| Metric | Meaning | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IS (Inception Score) | How clear and varied the generated images are | 3.52 |
| FID (Fréchet Inception Distance) | How close the generated images are to real photos; lower is better | 23.3 |
| MS-SSIM | How closely the restored image resembles the original | 0.878 |
| mAP | Average accuracy of artifact detection | 68.61 |
| Recognition rate | Proportion of correct artifact classifications | 92.43% |
