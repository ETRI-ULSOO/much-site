## In Preparation

Among the features of the MUCH (Multi-purpose Universal Cultural Heritage) platform, the ones that can be tried directly in a browser are selected and placed here. Two are being prepared.

### 3D Gaussian Splatting Viewer

Lets you rotate a 3D scene generated from multiple photographs using the mouse. Artifacts can be examined from any direction.

### Knowledge Graph Explorer

Displays the relationships among artifacts, periods, materials, and techniques as a graph, and lets you click through nodes of interest to follow them.

## In the Meantime

Until the demos are ready, you can watch videos covering the same features on the [Research](/much/research/) page.
