## Project Structure

The project was funded by the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and led by the Electronics and Telecommunications Research Institute. Two universities took part as participating organizations, dividing the research between them, and the National Museum of Korea took part as the end-user institution, raising requirements from the field and joining in the validation work.

## Participating Organizations

### Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism

The ministry that funded the project.

### Electronics and Telecommunications Research Institute (Lead)

Led the project as a whole. Its Content Convergence Research Lab, part of the Hyper-realistic Metaverse Research Institute, was responsible for platform architecture design, knowledge graph construction, and AI feature development.

### Korea National University of Cultural Heritage

Researched the standards and procedures for producing cultural heritage data.

### Chung-Ang University

Researched data visualization and content applications.

### National Museum of Korea (End-User Institution)

Raised the requirements needed in the field and confirmed that the research results could be used in actual exhibitions and collection management. The digital Gwanggaeto Stele installed on the "Road of History" is one result of this work.

## Research and Development Budget

The R&D budget was covered entirely by government funding, with no participating companies.
