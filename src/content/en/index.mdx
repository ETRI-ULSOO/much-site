hero:
  heading: A Sharing Platform That Connects Cultural Heritage Digitally
  lead: MUCH set standards for turning cultural heritage into digital data, and built a platform that let many fields — from exhibitions and education to content production — share that data. It started from a need raised by the National Museum of Korea and ran from April 2023 to December 2025. By the time the research concluded, it had led to the enactment of one domestic standard and left behind 12,131 items of composable data.
  facts:
    - The project's official title was "Development of Intelligent Heritage Sharing Platform Technology for Leading Cultural Heritage Digital Standards."
    - MUCH is short for Multi-purpose Universal Cultural Heritage.
